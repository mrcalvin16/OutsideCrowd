# OutsideCrowd Legal Policy Launch Inputs

The footer currently links to Privacy, Terms, and Refund pages that do not exist. Publishing legal language without confirmed business inputs can create obligations OutsideCrowd did not intend. These routes remain a launch blocker until the following fields are approved.

## Business identity

- legal entity name;
- business mailing address;
- support and privacy contact emails;
- governing law and venue;
- effective date;
- minimum user age and whether minors may attend or transact.

## Marketplace terms

- OutsideCrowd’s role as marketplace, merchant, or organizer agent;
- organizer eligibility and verification rules;
- platform fee and payment-processing treatment;
- prohibited events and content;
- event cancellation responsibilities;
- account suspension and appeal process;
- intellectual-property and flyer-upload permissions;
- limitation of liability and dispute-resolution language approved by counsel.

## Refund policy

- default refund rule;
- organizer-specific override rules;
- treatment of platform and processing fees;
- canceled, postponed, and materially changed events;
- refund request deadline and communication channel;
- processing timeline;
- chargeback handling;
- complimentary, manual, merchandise, and promoted-event purchases.

## Privacy policy

- categories of attendee, organizer, payment, device, and analytics data collected;
- purposes and lawful bases for processing;
- retention and deletion periods;
- user access, correction, deletion, and opt-out procedures;
- cookie and tracking configuration;
- marketing communications and consent;
- state or country privacy rights applicable to the launch market;
- subprocessors, including Clerk, Convex, Stripe, Vercel, OpenAI, and the external flyer image-generation service;
- AI data disclosures for AI Organizer and Flyer Studio;
- international data transfers;
- security incident contact and notification process.

## Required review

1. Product owner completes the business inputs.
2. Counsel drafts or approves the policy language.
3. Engineering creates `/privacy`, `/terms`, and `/refund-policy` using the approved copy.
4. Accessibility and mobile QA verify every policy route.
5. The effective date and version are recorded for future updates.

This checklist is operational planning, not legal advice.
